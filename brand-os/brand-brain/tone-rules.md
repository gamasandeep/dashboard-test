# Tone rules

How the voice flexes by situation. Voice stays the same; tone moves.

| Situation | Tone |
|---|---|
| Educational post | Curious, clear, a little playful |
| Promo | Calm and specific. No urgency words |
| Bad review reply | Warm, accountable, short. Take it offline |
| DM from a new lead | Friendly, one question back, no pressure |

## Banned phrases
- act now
- limited time
- last chance
- guaranteed results
- miracle
- cure
