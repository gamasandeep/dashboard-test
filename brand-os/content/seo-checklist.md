# SEO checklist

The Content Engine's SEO pass scores every draft against this list (each item is worth points).

- Primary keyword in the hook or title
- Primary keyword in the first 100 words
- At least one secondary keyword
- Answers the question within the first two sentences
- Readable: average sentence under 20 words
- Has a call to action
- Captions: 3–5 relevant hashtags; blogs: a meta description of 140–160 characters
