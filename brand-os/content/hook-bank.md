# Hook bank

Hook patterns that worked. The Content Engine picks a pattern and writes a fresh hook in the voice.

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Nobody warns you about X | "Nobody warns you about week 3." |
| You don't need X, you need Y | "Your skin doesn't need 12 steps. It needs the right 3." |
| The thing we'd actually do | "Here's what we'd actually put on your shelf." |
| Myth vs what really happens | "Purging isn't a breakout. Here's how to tell." |
