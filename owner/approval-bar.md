# Approval bar

_What ships without the owner and what waits for a yes._
OWNER INPUT · human-led · not counted as an agent.

## Ships on its own
- Routine content that passes the voice check and stays inside the budget.

## Waits for the owner (goes to `queue/needs-your-ok.md`)
- Budget changes
- Edge cases
- Anything that reads like a promise (prices, guarantees, results, availability)
- A reply to a competitor's promo
