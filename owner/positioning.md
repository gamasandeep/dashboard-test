# Positioning

_Who we are for, and why us._
OWNER INPUT · human-led · not counted as an agent.

- **For:** (who the customer is)
- **Who need:** (the problem they have)
- **We are:** (the category)
- **That:** (the main benefit)
- **Unlike:** (the main alternative)
- **We:** (the one thing only we do)

_Example: For busy people with sensitive skin who want results without a 12-step routine, we are a skincare studio
that builds a 3-step routine around your skin and checks in at week 3. Unlike big-box beauty counters, we only
recommend what we would use ourselves._
